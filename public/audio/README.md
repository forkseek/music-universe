# Music World listening room

Three original instrumental sketches are generated by `scripts/generate-listening-demos.mjs`.
The compositions and synthesized recordings are part of this project. No third-party audio,
platform audio URLs, decryption, authentication, or membership workarounds are used.

Each file is an 80-second mono PCM WAV at 32 kHz. They are separate from the existing
metadata-only Demo catalog. Local music selected in the player is played in the browser;
the player does not upload those files or persist their temporary blob URLs.
